## Ch06 · Loops

A program often has to repeat work: reading lines until the user quits, checking every element of a list, or counting through a range of values. This chapter covers the loops C offers for that, and the two statements that change how any loop runs. The `for` loop is the one you'll use most with the arrays from the last chapter, since it visits each element by its index.

The steps are:

* [While loops](#goto/01-while-loops): `while` and `do-while`, and the difference in when the condition is checked.
* [For loops](#goto/02-for-loops): counting loops, changing the step, and nested loops.
* [Looping over arrays](#goto/03-array-loops): visiting each element of an array by its index, and the size check that goes with it.
* [Break and continue](#goto/04-break-and-continue): stopping a loop early, and skipping to its next pass.
* [Battleship](#goto/05-battleship): a complete game that uses a two-dimensional array, a loop, and the checks from this chapter.
* [Summary](#goto/06-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
