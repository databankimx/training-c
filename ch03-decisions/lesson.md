## Ch03 · Decisions

A program that does the same thing every time isn't much use. Decisions let it choose what to do based on the values it has. This chapter covers the true and false values C uses, the operators that combine them, and the two statements that act on them: `if`, and `switch`.

The steps are:

* [Booleans](#goto/01-booleans): true and false, and the comparison operators that produce them.
* [Logical operators](#goto/02-logical-operators): combining conditions with `&&`, `||`, and `!`.
* [If statements](#goto/03-if-statements): running code only when a condition holds, and choosing between two paths.
* [Switch](#goto/04-switch): choosing one of several paths by a value, and the `break` that ends each one.
* [Summary](#goto/05-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.

---

*This chapter is written for this course. Booleans are described in the C17 standard, in the header `stdbool.h`.*
